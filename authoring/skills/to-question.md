# Ask Your Own Questions

Read [the workspace contract](../references/workspace.md) and [the dialogue contract](../references/dialogue.md) before accessing state. Both references are required for standalone use; sibling skills are not prerequisites.

1. **Select and prepare.** Reuse or initialize the main topic, objective, specific topic, and sources through the workspace contract. Read relevant sources; resolve inaccessible or partial scope explicitly. Reuse existing concept IDs or register source-grounded concepts without marking them assessed. Follow the active-work guard and migration rules.
2. **Start or resume.** Resume this topic's active `to-question` learning record, or create one through the dialogue contract. Invite the learner to formulate a why-question, comparison, or counterfactual in their own words. Offer a question pattern if they need help, not a finished list that replaces their effort. Save the exact invitation and wait.
3. **Investigate.** Save the learner's actual question before responding. When their reasoning is absent, ask what they predict or which connection they are trying to understand, save that exact prompt, and yield. Answer only after their response or explicit choice to decline that reasoning step. Then use inspected evidence, separating source claims, supported inferences, and unresolved hypotheticals. Mark external additions explicitly and obtain authorization first. Do not present an unsupported counterfactual as a fact.
4. **Record and continue.** Save tutor responses and concept-linked gaps or insights through the dialogue contract. Invite another learner question or their decision to end; never invent their reasoning or continue the dialogue on their behalf. Use `not_assessed` when there is no actual reasoning to evaluate. Persist the prompt and next action before waiting.
5. **Close explicitly.** On the learner's decision to finish, follow session finalization and the shared continuation rule. A pause preserves unfinished work. A requested skip is distinct from a completed dialogue and requires a saved reason.
