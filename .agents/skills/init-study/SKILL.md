# Initialize or Resume the Study Flow

Read [the workspace contract](../_study/workspace.md) and [the full-flow contract](../_study/flow.md) before accessing state. Resolve bundled references from this skill's location and the learner workspace separately. For review offers/enrollment also read [review.md](../_study/review.md).

1. **Locate and establish the mission.** Use an explicitly selected workspace, otherwise `study/` under the learner's working directory. Inspect existing states before creating one; resolve ambiguous main-topic choices. Reuse a selected objective/language or ask only for missing information. Initialize once those are known, even without a specific topic. Confirm changes to an existing objective, preserving its previous value in history. Apply privacy, source-integrity, schema-version, and consented-migration rules before writes.
2. **Resume navigation first.** If `flow.pending_prompt` records an unresolved due-review choice, restore it instead of generating a new offer. If a review excursion is active, route it through the review owner and reconcile/restore its suspended checkpoint through the full-flow contract before selecting another activity. Do not lose a pending learner answer behind a new navigation prompt.
3. **Offer due reviews on entry.** On a fresh explicit invocation or workspace reopening, display enrolled topics whose saved due times have passed and ask whether to review before continuing study. Save this navigation choice separately from topic checkpoints. A decline leaves dates and pending work intact. Active-work conflicts are explained rather than silently abandoned. Do not repeat the startup offer after every learner reply in an ongoing dialogue.
4. **Select or resume a specific topic.** Accept a new topic/material progressively, or reuse an existing topic ID. If the learner has unfinished work, show its exact prompt and next action and honor their resume/switch decision. Keep other topics' checkpoints intact. Route active practice, review, dialogue, or export to its owning skill; multi-topic reservations may have another owner. Starting the full flow records orchestrated mode and an actual cycle start time if absent, without resetting completed work or schedules.
5. **Drive the dispatch loop.** Follow the full-flow contract's next-step selection and completion gates. Activate each required method through the host's native mechanism, passing the same resolved workspace/main-topic/specific-topic IDs and orchestrated mode. Read its authoritative instructions rather than copying its procedure here. On method return, reread persisted state: continue agent-only work, or save and yield at a real learner question, source gap, permission prompt, pause, or skip decision. Missing/denied skill activation is a blocker, never a fabricated completion or a consent bypass.
6. **Close the initial cycle, not the review clock.** Once the first six gates pass, use the full-flow finalization protocol to record completion and enroll for the first delayed review. Report the actual due date and remaining gaps. Do not run a cumulative quiz immediately. Accept the next specific topic or pause; later due reviews use `to-review` across the enrolled pool.

This is an interactive agent-driven loop, not a deterministic runtime, background job, or promise of mastery. Preserve all real learner contributions and never simulate the work required to pass a gate.
