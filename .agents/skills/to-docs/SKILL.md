# Export Study Documents

Read [the workspace contract](../_study/workspace.md) and [the export contract](../_study/exports.md) before accessing state. Standalone use needs the bundled references, not completion of prior methods.

1. **Select the scope.** Reuse the supplied workspace/main-topic IDs or initialize through the workspace contract. In orchestrated mode default to the current specific topic. Independently accept an explicit list of one or several specific topics within one main topic; resolve ambiguous names before writing. If a request spans main topics, ask for separate exports rather than silently combining independent states. Record the selected topic IDs and an owner topic through the export contract.
2. **Resume or prepare.** Resume a matching unfinished export before allocating another pair. Respect the active-work guard across every selected topic. Actually inspect underlying sources, not just generated maps or feedback. Ask for readable representations or accepted partial scope when necessary. If no relevant material is readable for a selected topic, block or obtain an explicit narrower topic selection; never silently omit that topic.
3. **Reserve the pair.** Allocate and save the export record, its two new workspace-confined filenames, and checkpoint before generation. Follow the export contract for artifact-first writes, interruption, orphan reconciliation, and nonoverwriting versions. Preserve sources, prior exports, and unrelated checkpoints.
4. **Write both documents.** Follow the two document formats in the export contract. Keep topic boundaries and source evidence visible; include only supported cross-topic connections. Identify uncertainties, contradictory sources, and authorized external additions. The textual map must be readable without Mermaid; the explanation must stand alone for a third-party reader.
5. **Finalize together.** Inspect both readable files and provenance, register their linked artifacts, and finalize the export only when both meet the contract. Report the two paths and selected scope. Do not expose internal snapshots or metadata as a third primary export. Follow the export continuation rule without enrolling reviews or marking other topics learned.

Do not generate or upload audio/video, promise compatibility with every media service, or use external tools without the corresponding permission. These documents are study inputs, not evidence of learner understanding.
